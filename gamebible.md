We are building an online game that is a cross between motherlode and any of the online multiplayer games like travian & co.

Game idea: mining.
You need to reach the core.
You start with a super basic equipment and you can upgrade it to dig faster and deeper.
You can also build structures on the surface to help you mine.
Moreover you can expand the mining operation in 3 different ways:
1. an engineer: can build structures on the surface and in a mined tile to help you mine.
2. a miner: can dig faster and deeper.
3. a scientist: can research new technologies to help you mine.

The rig itself has (expandable):
- a crew
- a drill
- an operator.
The rig will also have some equipment slots that can be filled with different equipment or multiple crews (to either balance the cons of one crew or to enhance the pros of another crew).

You can, as the game progresses, recruit different crews that have pros and cons.
E.g. a crew of Intelligent Monkeys can dig faster in the soil but will be slower in the bananium.

You can also upgrade the drill and the operator.

As the game progresses, you will encounter different types of rocks and minerals that you need to mine.
Some of them are easy to mine, others are hard to mine.
Some of them are valuable, others are less.

There are also other players that you can interact with.
- You can trade with them.
- You cannot attack them.
- You can form alliances with them.

There are also events that can happen in the game.
- Natural disasters.
- Meteor showers.
- Alien invasions.
- monsters, traps and natural hazards while mining.

You need to reach the core to retrieve a revelation. This revelation needs to be brought back to the surface to win the game.
The twist: you cannot return to the surface alone. You need to find other players to help you.
when the revelation is retrieved the winning crew(s) will be announced and the game will end, triggering a world restart.

The mining operation will span over a large area of land. 
Every time you select a tile, you claim it. Once claimed, you'll span a time to prospect it. Based on the current crew, surface operation and research, the time will be shortened. After the prospecting is done, you'll see what's in the tile. You can then start mining it. Note: start mine is an action that will turn on the "mining timer" at the end of which the minerals/resources will start the transport towards the closest node or base. Not a clicker game :)
If you cannot mine it, you can release it and someone else can claim it.

There are different types of tiles:
- Soil: easy to mine, low value.
- Rocks: harder to mine, medium value.
- Minerals: hard to mine, high value.
- Bananium/Other various fantasy/techy names: very hard to mine, very high value.
Every tile is ideally minable, but the deeper you go, the harder it gets and the more time it will cost to mine. Example: if the starting equipment is just a shovel, you can dig soil with "normal" speed but chipping away at stones will cost 10x as much time and exponentially so for the rest of the materials. Meaning that you can dig bananium with a shovel, but it will take you a very long time. 

There are also different types of resources:
- Water: needed for the crew to survive.
- Food: needed for the crew to survive.
- Energy: needed for the drill to work.
- Fuel: needed for the drill to work.
- Other various resources that can be used for crafting and upgrading.


The world map is shared between all players. Any player can claim a tile, but only one player can claim a tile at a time. The claimed tile will be marked as claimed and will be visible to all players. The distance between tiles and the base operation is calculated using the Manhattan distance and will impact the time it takes to travel between them. Dug tiles can be used to build structures on them, e.g. a logistic relay (shorter distance to deposit resources) or a service station to repair/upgrade/refill the drill. 

There is no permanent death - a dry drill will be returned empty to the base operation and the crew will be returned to the surface. 
Potentially this will cost some currency as a "rescue fee" to discourage reckless play. 

There will be a "market" where players can trade resources with each other.
